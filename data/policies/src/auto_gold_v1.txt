AutoShield Gold Motor Insurance
Policy Wording | Product Version AUTO-GOLD-V1

This document sets out the terms on which the insurer will indemnify the insured for loss of or damage to the insured vehicle. Please read it together with the policy schedule, which states the sum insured, the policy period and the inception date that apply to your policy.

1. Definitions
Words and expressions in this policy have the meanings given below wherever they appear in bold or with initial capitals.

1.1 Insured Vehicle
The Insured Vehicle means the private car described in the policy schedule, including its standard fittings and accessories supplied by the manufacturer, while it is registered for private use in the name of the insured.

1.2 Sum Insured
The Sum Insured is the maximum amount payable under this policy for all claims arising from any single incident. It is stated in the policy schedule and is based on the insured declared value of the vehicle at the inception date.

1.3 Incident
An Incident means a single sudden and unforeseen event, or a series of events arising from one originating cause, that results in loss of or damage to the Insured Vehicle during the policy period.

1.4 Waiting Period
The Waiting Period means the period of fifteen days starting on the inception date during which no claim is payable, as described in clause 3.4 of this policy.

2. Scope of Cover
Subject to the conditions and exclusions of this policy, the insurer will pay for loss of or damage to the Insured Vehicle caused by the perils listed in this section.

2.1 Collision Damage
The insurer will pay the reasonable cost of repair or replacement of the Insured Vehicle following a collision with another vehicle, a stationary object or an animal, including damage caused while the vehicle is parked. Repairs are paid up to the Sum Insured, less the compulsory deductible of INR 2,500 for each claim.

2.2 Theft of the Vehicle
The insurer will pay for the loss of the Insured Vehicle by theft, or for damage caused by an attempted theft, up to the Sum Insured. Cover for theft applies only if the condition on police reporting in clause 3.2 is met and the vehicle was locked with its keys removed at the time of the theft.

2.3 Natural Calamities
The insurer will pay for damage caused by flood, inundation, cyclone, storm, hailstorm, landslide or earthquake. The claim must be supported by a report of the weather or seismic event from a recognised public authority or meteorological department.

2.4 Fire and Explosion
The insurer will pay for loss of or damage to the Insured Vehicle caused by accidental fire, explosion, self-ignition or lightning, including damage caused by efforts to extinguish the fire.

2.5 Malicious Damage
The insurer will pay for damage caused deliberately by third parties, such as vandalism, riot or civil commotion, provided the incident is reported to the police within forty-eight hours of discovery.

2.6 Third-Party Liability
The insurer will indemnify the insured against legal liability for death of or bodily injury to any person, and for damage to third-party property, arising out of the use of the Insured Vehicle, as required by the applicable motor vehicles legislation.

3. Conditions of Cover
The insurer's liability depends on the insured complying with the conditions in this section.

3.1 Notification of Claims
The insured must notify the insurer of any incident likely to give rise to a claim within seven days of the incident, and must not authorise repairs, other than those needed to make the vehicle safe, before the insurer has inspected the damage.

3.2 Police Report for Theft
For any claim for theft or attempted theft, a First Information Report must be filed with the police within forty-eight hours of discovering the loss, and a copy must be provided to the insurer. Where the vehicle is not recovered within ninety days, the insurer will settle the claim as a total loss.

3.3 Supporting Documents
Every claim must be supported by the claim form, a copy of the registration certificate, the driving licence of the person driving at the time of the incident, repair estimates or invoices, and photographs of the damage where available.

3.4 Waiting Period
No claim is payable for any incident occurring during the first fifteen days after the inception date of the policy. This waiting period applies to every type of claim under this policy and is not waived for renewals with a break in cover.

3.5 Limit of Liability
The total amount payable for any single incident will not exceed the Sum Insured stated in the policy schedule. Any claimed amount above the Sum Insured is not payable under this policy.

3.6 Network Garages
Cashless repairs are available at the insurer's network garages. Where the insured chooses a non-network garage, the insurer will reimburse the reasonable cost of repairs on submission of the original invoice.

4. Exclusions
The insurer will not pay for any loss, damage or liability described in this section, whatever its cause.

4.1 Racing and Speed Testing
Loss or damage occurring while the Insured Vehicle is being used for racing, rallies, pace-making, speed testing or reliability trials, on public roads or on a closed track, is excluded.

4.2 Commercial and Ride-Hailing Use
Loss or damage occurring while the Insured Vehicle is used for hire or reward, for carrying passengers or goods commercially, or on any ride-hailing or delivery platform, is excluded unless such use is declared and accepted in the policy schedule.

4.3 Driving Under the Influence
Loss or damage occurring while the vehicle is driven by a person under the influence of alcohol or drugs beyond the legal limit is excluded.

4.4 Unlicensed Driver
Loss or damage occurring while the vehicle is driven by a person who does not hold a valid driving licence for that class of vehicle is excluded.

4.5 Wear and Tear
Mechanical or electrical breakdown, wear and tear, depreciation, and damage to tyres unless the vehicle is damaged at the same time are excluded.

4.6 Consequential Loss
Loss of use of the vehicle, hire of a replacement vehicle, loss of earnings and any other consequential loss are excluded.

5. Claims Settlement
This section explains how the insurer assesses and pays claims.

5.1 Assessment of Loss
The insurer will appoint a surveyor to assess every claim above INR 50,000. The amount payable is the cost of repair or replacement, less depreciation on replaced parts as set out in the policy schedule and less the compulsory deductible.

5.2 Partial Settlement
Where part of a claim is covered and part is excluded, for example where a claimed repair invoice includes both accident damage and wear and tear, the insurer will pay only the covered part of the claim.

5.3 Total Loss
Where the cost of repair exceeds seventy-five percent of the Sum Insured, or the vehicle is stolen and not recovered, the claim is settled as a total loss at the Sum Insured.

5.4 Fraudulent Claims
If any claim is fraudulent or exaggerated, or supported by a false declaration or document, the insurer will not pay the claim and may cancel the policy from the date of the fraudulent act.

6. General Provisions
The following general provisions apply to the whole policy.

6.1 Cancellation
The insured may cancel the policy at any time by written notice. The insurer will refund the premium for the unexpired period on a short-period scale, provided no claim has been made during the policy period.

6.2 Grievance Redressal
Complaints about a claim decision can be raised with the insurer's grievance officer. Every claim decision states the clauses relied upon, and the insured may ask for a review by a claims officer.
