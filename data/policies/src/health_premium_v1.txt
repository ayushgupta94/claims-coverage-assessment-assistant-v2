CareFirst Premium Health Insurance
Policy Wording | Product Version HEALTH-PREMIUM-V1

This policy covers medical expenses incurred by the insured person for treatment taken in India during the policy period. It must be read together with the policy schedule, which states the sum insured and the inception date of cover.

1. Definitions
The following words have the meaning given to them below wherever they appear in this policy.

1.1 Hospital
A Hospital means an institution registered with the local authorities for in-patient care and treatment, with at least ten in-patient beds, qualified nursing staff on duty around the clock, and a fully equipped operation theatre where surgical procedures are carried out.

1.2 Hospitalisation
Hospitalisation means admission to a Hospital as an in-patient for a continuous period of at least twenty-four hours on the advice of a medical practitioner, except for the day-care procedures described in clause 2.3 of this policy.

1.3 Medically Necessary Treatment
Medically Necessary Treatment means treatment that is required for the medical management of an illness or injury, follows accepted standards of medical practice, and is not provided primarily for the comfort or convenience of the insured person.

1.4 Pre-Existing Disease
A Pre-Existing Disease means any condition, ailment or injury that was diagnosed, or for which medical advice or treatment was received, within forty-eight months before the inception date of the first policy.

2. Scope of Cover
Subject to the terms, conditions and exclusions of this policy, the insurer will pay the following expenses up to the sum insured.

2.1 In-Patient Hospitalisation
The insurer will pay for room rent, nursing, intensive care, surgeon and consultant fees, anaesthesia, medicines, diagnostic tests and operation theatre charges incurred during Hospitalisation for Medically Necessary Treatment of an illness or injury.

2.2 Pre and Post Hospitalisation Expenses
The insurer will pay medical expenses incurred in the thirty days before Hospitalisation and the sixty days after discharge, provided they relate to the same condition for which the insured person was hospitalised and the Hospitalisation claim is admissible.

2.3 Day-Care Procedures
The insurer will pay for listed day-care procedures, such as cataract surgery, dialysis and chemotherapy, that need less than twenty-four hours of admission because of advances in medical technology.

2.4 Emergency Ambulance
The insurer will pay up to INR 3,000 for each Hospitalisation towards road ambulance charges for transporting the insured person to the nearest Hospital in a medical emergency.

2.5 Organ Donor Expenses
The insurer will pay the in-patient expenses of harvesting an organ from a donor for transplantation to the insured person, where the transplant itself is covered under this policy.

3. Waiting Periods and Conditions
The cover described in section 2 is subject to the waiting periods and conditions in this section.

3.1 Initial Waiting Period
No claim is payable for any treatment or Hospitalisation that begins during the first ninety days after the inception date of the policy. This initial waiting period applies to every type of claim, including claims arising from accidental injury.

3.2 Pre-Existing Diseases
Expenses related to a Pre-Existing Disease are covered only after forty-eight months of continuous cover with the insurer, and only if the disease was declared in the proposal form.

3.3 Cashless Treatment
Cashless treatment is available at network Hospitals if the insurer's third-party administrator approves the pre-authorisation request before planned admission, or within twenty-four hours of an emergency admission.

3.4 Reimbursement Claims
For reimbursement claims, the insured person must submit the claim form, the discharge summary, original bills and receipts, investigation reports and the prescriptions within thirty days of discharge.

3.5 Sum Insured Limit
The total amount payable for all claims made during the policy period will not exceed the sum insured stated in the policy schedule. Any expense above the sum insured is not payable.

4. Exclusions
The insurer will not pay for any expenses arising from or related to the following.

4.1 Cosmetic and Aesthetic Procedures
Cosmetic, aesthetic or plastic surgery, including procedures to change appearance such as liposuction, hair transplantation or rhinoplasty, is excluded unless it is needed to reconstruct the body after an accident, burns or cancer surgery.

4.2 Self-Inflicted Injury
Treatment of intentional self-inflicted injury or attempted suicide is excluded, unless required by the applicable mental healthcare legislation.

4.3 Experimental Treatment
Unproven, experimental or investigational treatments, devices and medicines that are not supported by established medical practice are excluded.

4.4 Substance Abuse
Treatment for alcoholism, drug or substance abuse, or any addictive condition, and its consequences, is excluded.

4.5 Dental Treatment
Dental treatment, dentures and dental implants are excluded unless they need Hospitalisation because of an accidental injury.

4.6 Outpatient Consultations
Outpatient consultations, health check-ups and medicines bought without Hospitalisation are excluded, except as covered under pre and post Hospitalisation expenses.

5. Claims Settlement
This section explains how the amount payable is determined.

5.1 Room Rent Limit
Room rent is payable up to one percent of the sum insured per day. If the insured person stays in a room with a higher rent, all associated medical expenses are paid in the same proportion as the eligible room rent bears to the actual room rent.

5.2 Co-payment
For insured persons aged sixty-one years or more at the inception date, each admissible claim is subject to a co-payment of ten percent, which is borne by the insured person.

5.3 Partial Settlement
Where a bill includes both covered and excluded items, such as an admission for a covered surgery that also includes a cosmetic procedure, only the covered items are payable.

5.4 Fraudulent Claims
If a claim is fraudulent, or is supported by any false document or statement, the claim will not be paid and all benefits under this policy will be forfeited.

6. General Provisions
The following general provisions apply to the whole policy.

6.1 Free Look Period
The insured may review the policy within fifteen days of receiving it and cancel it if not satisfied, provided no claim has been made.

6.2 Grievance Redressal
The insured may raise a complaint about any claim decision with the insurer's grievance cell. Every decision letter names the clauses of this policy on which the decision is based.
