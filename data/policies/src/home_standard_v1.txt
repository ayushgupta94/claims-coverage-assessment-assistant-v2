HomeSecure Standard Household Insurance
Policy Wording | Product Version HOME-STANDARD-V1

This policy protects the insured's home building and its contents against the perils described below. It must be read together with the policy schedule, which states the address of the insured home, the sum insured and the inception date.

1. Definitions
The following words have the meaning given to them below wherever they appear in this policy.

1.1 Home
The Home means the residential building at the address in the policy schedule, including its permanent fixtures and fittings, boundary walls and gates, used solely as a private dwelling.

1.2 Contents
Contents means household goods and personal belongings of the insured and family members living with them, kept inside the Home, but excluding cash, securities, vehicles and goods held for business purposes.

1.3 Unoccupied
The Home is Unoccupied when it has not been lived in by the insured or a family member for more than thirty consecutive days.

2. Scope of Cover
Subject to the conditions and exclusions of this policy, the insurer will pay for physical loss of or damage to the Home and its Contents caused by the following perils.

2.1 Fire and Lightning
The insurer will pay for loss or damage caused by fire, lightning, explosion or implosion, including smoke damage and damage caused by fire brigade efforts to put out the fire.

2.2 Burst Pipes and Water Damage
The insurer will pay for damage caused by water escaping suddenly from burst or overflowing water tanks, pipes or apparatus, including the cost of tracing the source of the leak. Damage caused by water seeping gradually over a long period is not covered.

2.3 Storm and Flood
The insurer will pay for damage caused by storm, cyclone, typhoon, tempest, hurricane, tornado, flood or inundation, provided the event is confirmed by the meteorological department or a recognised public authority.

2.4 Burglary and Theft
The insurer will pay for loss of or damage to Contents caused by burglary or theft involving forcible and violent entry into or exit from the Home, provided the incident is reported to the police within twenty-four hours of discovery.

2.5 Earthquake
The insurer will pay for loss or damage caused by earthquake, volcanic eruption or other convulsions of nature, including resulting fire and landslide.

2.6 Accidental Breakage of Glass
The insurer will pay for accidental breakage of fixed glass in windows, doors and fanlights of the Home, including the cost of temporary boarding up.

3. Conditions of Cover
The insurer's liability depends on the insured complying with the conditions in this section.

3.1 Unoccupied Home
Cover for burglary, theft, malicious damage and water damage stops if the Home is Unoccupied, unless the insurer has agreed in writing to continue cover during the period of non-occupation.

3.2 Security Precautions
All doors and windows must be secured with the locks fitted when the Home is left empty. A claim for burglary will not be paid if entry was gained through a door or window left unlocked.

3.3 Notification of Claims
The insured must notify the insurer within fourteen days of any incident, take all reasonable steps to prevent further damage, and keep damaged items available for inspection.

3.4 Sum Insured and Under-Insurance
The amount payable for any one incident will not exceed the sum insured stated in the policy schedule. If the sum insured is less than the replacement value of the property, the claim is reduced in the same proportion.

4. Exclusions
The insurer will not pay for loss, damage or liability described in this section.

4.1 War and Terrorism
Loss or damage caused directly or indirectly by war, invasion, act of foreign enemy, hostilities, civil war, rebellion, revolution, military or usurped power, or any act of terrorism, is excluded.

4.2 Nuclear Risks
Loss or damage caused by ionising radiation, radioactive contamination, nuclear fuel or nuclear waste, or by nuclear weapons material, is excluded.

4.3 Gradual Deterioration
Wear and tear, rust, rot, mould, gradual deterioration, settlement or shrinkage of the building, and damage caused by a lack of maintenance are excluded.

4.4 Wilful Damage
Loss or damage caused deliberately by the insured, a family member or any person living in the Home is excluded.

4.5 Pests and Vermin
Damage caused by insects, termites, rodents, pets or vermin is excluded.

5. Claims Settlement
This section explains how the insurer settles claims.

5.1 Basis of Settlement
Damage to the building is paid on a reinstatement basis, meaning the cost of repair to a condition equal to, but not better than, its condition when new. Contents are paid at replacement cost less an allowance for wear and tear.

5.2 Partial Settlement
Where a loss includes both covered and excluded damage, for example sudden water damage together with damage caused by long-term seepage, only the covered part of the loss is payable.

5.3 Fraudulent Claims
If a claim is fraudulent or exaggerated in any respect, the insurer will not pay the claim and may cancel the policy without refund of premium.

6. General Provisions
The following general provisions apply to the whole policy.

6.1 Cancellation
The insured may cancel the policy at any time by written notice, and the insurer will refund premium for the unexpired period on a short-period scale.

6.2 Grievance Redressal
The insured may raise a complaint about any claim decision with the insurer's grievance officer. Every decision states the clauses relied upon.
